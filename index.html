The Journey

Every Journey Begins with a Purpose

Success is rarely an overnight achievement. It is built through resilience, continuous learning, and the courage to embrace every challenge.

The entrepreneurial journey began with a simple observation while working in the hospitality industry. He witnessed the lack of professionalism, inconsistent service, and operational challenges within the event rental industry. Rather than accepting these limitations, he envisioned a better way — one built on trust, quality, and reliability.

With limited financial resources but unwavering determination, he established Impress by starting with rented equipment, a small warehouse, and an unwavering commitment to delivering exceptional service. Every completed project became an opportunity to reinvest, innovate, and expand. What started as a single business gradually evolved into a complete ecosystem of specialized event ventures serving corporate organizations, institutions, weddings, exhibitions, and government events across Tamil Nadu.

For Mathew, entrepreneurship has never been about building a company alone—it has always been about creating opportunities, empowering people, and setting new standards for the industry.
